Last Supper

Medium
Oil on Canvas
Year
2026
Format
60"x24"

About

This work depicts the Last Supper, approached through the lens of dramatic sacred narrative. The composition began with extensive research and preparatory sketches, using 3D models to help resolve the arrangement of figures, spatial depth, and lighting before committing to a final design. Once the drawing was finalized, it was transferred to the canvas, where the painting was built through a grisaille underpainting to establish value structure and form, followed by direct paint layers to develop color, texture, and final resolution.
